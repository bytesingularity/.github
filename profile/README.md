# ByteSingularity Studios

An independent studio building multiplayer games. Our first is **Portfall**.

## Portfall

**A space MMO where the better pilot wins, and the galaxy remembers what you took.**

Portfall is a top-down space MMO where every fight is won on the stick. Out past the
ports, planets wait to be claimed, colonies grow while you're away, and every hold of
cargo crossing open space is somebody's target. Fly alone, or bring your company and
take a piece of the galaxy for yourselves.

**Status:** in development, private playtest on Steam (Windows).

Portfall's source is closed. It is built on MonoGame and .NET with a
server-authoritative simulation, and the devlog covers the engineering behind it.
